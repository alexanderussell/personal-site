## Challenge

The internal platform made provisioning fast, and that was the problem. A single click could
stand up real infrastructure that cost real money, and the interface gave that click exactly
as much weight as changing a display name.

## Approach

Rather than adding a confirmation dialog — which teaches people to click through — we made the
cost of the action visible inside the control itself, and made the commitment proportional to
the consequence.

## Solution

A provisioning flow that shows projected cost and blast radius before the commit, and a
press-and-hold control on the destructive path. Everyday actions stayed one click.

## Outcome

Accidental provisions dropped by about half without slowing the common path.
