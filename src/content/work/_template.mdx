## Challenge

What was actually hard. State the problem as the business or the user experienced it, not
as a design brief. If there's a number that shows the pain, lead with it.

## Approach

How you decided what to do. This is the section that distinguishes a designer from a
portfolio — what you ruled out and why matters more than what you shipped.

## Solution

What you built. Be specific about your own contribution when the work was collaborative;
vague collective voice reads as hiding something.

## Outcome

What changed as a result. Prefer measured over asserted. If nothing was measured, say what
you observed and label it as such — an honest qualitative result beats an invented metric.

## Project details

- **Team:** who else was involved and in what role
- **Timeline:** how long it actually took
- **Tools:** what you worked in
