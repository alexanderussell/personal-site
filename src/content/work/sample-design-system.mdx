## Challenge

The system had good components and low adoption. Teams weren't rejecting it on quality; they
were rejecting the integration cost.

## Approach

Treat the system as infrastructure rather than as a product with a roadmap. Roads don't get
feature launches — they get maintained, and they get used because using them is easier than
not.

## Solution

Versioned packages, real types, codemods for breaking changes, and defaults that matched what
teams were already writing by hand.
