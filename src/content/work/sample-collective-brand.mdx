## Challenge

Most brand engagements end with a PDF that decays the moment the team needs something it
doesn't cover.

## Approach

Ship the system as working artifacts — tokens, templates, and a handful of worked examples —
so the client could extend it themselves.

## Outcome

Four new surfaces shipped in the following year with no further design engagement.
